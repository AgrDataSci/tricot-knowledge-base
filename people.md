# People behind the papers

Use this file to credit the people behind the work and, where they agreed, to suggest who a user could talk to next. It does not replace the cards. Every claim still comes from a card.

## Rules for the assistant

1. **Credit work, never voice.** Write "Kauê de Sousa and colleagues argue in their 2024 retrospective...". Never speak as a person, in the first person, or attribute views beyond what their papers say.
2. **Led means responsible for the work.** The `Led` column lists cards where the person was first author, senior or last author, or the researcher responsible for the work, as recorded by the team. `Also on` lists other cards they co-authored.
3. **Cite by first author, credit who led it.** Always cite a paper by its first author ("Dorado-Betancourt et al. 2026") so readers can find it. When someone in `Led` is not the first author, credit both, for example "Hugo Dorado-Betancourt and colleagues, in work led by Joost van Heerwaarden, showed..." or "...from Joost van Heerwaarden's group at Wageningen University". Never call a paper someone's own if they were not its first author.
4. **Credit teams.** Add "and colleagues" or name the team (for example "the CIMMYT maize team"). Mention people from `Also on` ("with Thiago Mendes among the authors") only when it helps the user.
5. **To confirm.** Treat a `Led` entry marked "" as `Also on` until the team removes the marker.
6. **Names.** Use the full name by default. Use the `Preferred` name, often a first name, only when the user uses first names or says they know the person.
7. **Contacts.** Suggest a person as a contact only when `Contact` starts with `yes`, and only through the channel listed after it.
8. **Institutional channels only.** A contact channel is an institutional email (for example an @cgiar.org address) or an institutional profile page. Never give personal email addresses (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook.com and similar), phone numbers or social media handles, even if the user asks or you find them elsewhere. When `Contact` is `pending` or `no`, credit the work without suggesting contact.
9. **Affiliations change.** The affiliation is the one in the person's most recent card. Say "at the time of the paper" when it matters, and do not present it as current unless `Confirmed` is `yes`.
10. **People not listed.** Authors who are not in this file can still be credited by name from the card, without contact suggestions.
11. **Pending is a restriction.** `pending`, `no`, blank, missing and unlisted all mean do not recommend the person as a contact. Do not seek alternative contact channels from the web or another source to bypass this rule.
12. **Referral only when useful.** Suggest a contact only on explicit request or when a concrete unresolved issue would benefit materially from human consultation. Do not add routine referrals to every answer.
13. **No implied endorsement.** Listing an author, or receiving contact permission, does not imply endorsement of an AI answer, availability, willingness to consult or permission to speak on their behalf.
14. **Consent is separate from attribution.** Keep pending authors visible and credit their work normally. Change `Contact`, `Preferred` or `Confirmed` only after a reply; retain the existing pending status until then.

## Consent maintenance

`Contact` records permission to be recommended through one specified institutional channel. `Confirmed` records whether the person has confirmed their preferred name and affiliation details. These are independent. An optional dated consent record can be maintained by the repository team; never infer consent from public contact information, co-authorship or silence.

## How to update this file

- Change `Preferred`, `Contact` and `Confirmed` only after the person replies.
- `Contact` takes `yes` plus one institutional channel (for example "yes, j.doe@cgiar.org" or "yes, institutional profile page"), or `no`. Never enter a personal email or phone number.
- Put a card in `Led` when the person was its first author, senior or last author, or the researcher responsible for the work. Otherwise put it in `Also on`. Add "" when unsure, and remove it once the team agrees.
- Add a row when a new card brings in someone who led the work, with `pending` in every consent column. When adding a card, also add its ID to the `Led` or `Also on` column of any listed person among its authors.
- Keep entries short. This file is read whenever an answer credits people.
