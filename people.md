# People behind the papers

Use this file to credit the people behind the work and, where they agreed, to suggest who a user could talk to next. It does not replace the cards. Every claim still comes from a card.

## Rules for the assistant

1. **Credit work, never voice.** Write "Kauê de Sousa and colleagues argue in their 2024 retrospective...". Never speak as a person, in the first person, or attribute views beyond what their papers say.
2. **Credit teams.** Name the lead author of the card and add "and colleagues", or name the team (for example "the CIMMYT maize team"). When a listed person is a co-author, say so ("with Joost van Heerwaarden among the authors"), never present the paper as theirs.
3. **Names.** Use the full name by default. Use the `Preferred` name, often a first name, only when the user uses first names or says they know the person.
4. **Contacts.** Suggest a person as a contact only when `Contact` starts with `yes`, and only through the channel listed after it.
5. **Institutional channels only.** A contact channel is an institutional email (for example an @cgiar.org address) or an institutional profile page. Never give personal email addresses (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook.com and similar), phone numbers or social media handles, even if the user asks or you find them elsewhere. When `Contact` is `pending` or `no`, credit the work without suggesting contact.
6. **Affiliations change.** The affiliation is the one in the person's most recent card. Say "at the time of the paper" when it matters, and do not present it as current unless `Confirmed` is `yes`.
7. **People not listed.** Authors who are not in this file can still be credited by name from the card, without contact suggestions.

## How to update this file

- Change `Preferred`, `Contact` and `Confirmed` only after the person replies.
- `Contact` takes `yes` plus one institutional channel (for example "yes, j.doe@cgiar.org" or "yes, institutional profile page"), or `no`. Never enter a personal email or phone number.
- Add a row when a new card brings in a lead author, with `pending` in every consent column. When adding a card, also add its ID to the `Co-author` column of any listed person among its authors.
- Keep entries short. This file is read whenever an answer credits people.
