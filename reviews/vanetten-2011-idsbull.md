The card is accurate and well written. Every number, method bullet and stated limitation matches the essay. The problems are of the kind this review was asked to watch for. The gist and two Summary sentences put the later tricot lineage into the paper's own voice ("first proposal of what became tricot", "design choices that tricot later kept"), which the 2011 essay cannot say. Note 5 goes the other way. It suggests that use for commercial products came only with later tricot work, but the essay itself already proposes that seed companies could have their varieties evaluated (p. 108). The most important fix is Note 5, then the gist and Summary sentence 1.

## Fixes needed

1. **Note 5 understates what the essay already proposes (Imprecise, changes meaning).**
   Card: "The essay frames crowdsourced evaluation as a way to target food crops and farmer varieties for household food security. Much later tricot work applies the approach to breeding pipelines and commercial products as well."
   Source: the main framing is food crops for household food security (p. 106). But the essay also says seed companies may want their own varieties, or even intermediate products, evaluated by crowdsourcing to get performance and market data, and that opening the network to commercial seeds could help finance it (p. 108). It also says the data could help calibrate public breeding priorities (p. 107).
   Replacement: "- The essay frames crowdsourced evaluation mainly as a way to target food crops and farmer varieties for household food security. It also foresees that seed companies could have their own varieties, or even intermediate products, evaluated by the network, which might help finance it, and that the data could help set public breeding priorities (pp. 107 to 108). Later tricot work applied the approach widely in breeding pipelines (outside this source)."

2. **Gist states outside knowledge as the paper's content (Imprecise).**
   Card: "The first proposal of what became tricot, arguing that ..."
   Source: the essay proposes crowdsourced seed innovation. It does not, and could not, say it became tricot (p. 102 to 103). The lineage is true outside knowledge and belongs in Notes. The keyword "tricot origin" can stay.
   Replacement: "gist: Proposes crowdsourced crop improvement in which many farmers grow small blind seed packages in triads, rank them and report by mobile phone, to scale up participatory variety evaluation" (28 words)

3. **Summary sentence 1 is not in the source (Not in source).**
   Card: "This short essay is the first published proposal of the approach that later became tricot."
   Source: nothing in the essay about later work (p. 102).
   Replacement: "This short essay proposes a crowdsourcing approach to crop improvement and seed innovation in sub-Saharan Africa."
   Add to Notes: "- The tricot team treats this essay as the first published proposal of the approach that later became tricot (outside this source)."

4. **Summary paragraph 3, first sentence, is not in the source (Not in source).**
   Card: "Several design choices that tricot later kept appear here."
   Source: the essay describes the design but cannot say what tricot kept. Some of these choices were later changed in practice (for example, Note 3 already says data collection moved to field agents and ClimMob forms).
   Replacement: "The proposal also sets out how farmers would take part."

5. **Note 3 sets up a contrast the source does not support (Imprecise).**
   Card: "The paper describes reporting after harvest by voice or SMS. In later tricot work, field agents register farmers and collect data ..."
   Source: the essay already allows questionnaires to be given with the help of a field agent when seed goes through extension services or NGOs, and proposes prior registration of each package code by free phone message (p. 104).
   Replacement: "- The paper proposes reporting after harvest through automated voice or SMS questionnaires, by farmers directly or with a field agent when seed goes through extension services or NGOs, after farmers register each package code by phone. In later tricot work, field agents register farmers and collect data through forms generated by ClimMob (see quiros-2024-compag)."

6. **Preference-learning sentence drops the source's condition (Imprecise, low).**
   Card: "Analysis would draw on preference learning, the field behind online recommendation systems, to rebuild a ranking of varieties and to find the environmental and social factors that shape preferences, using the location of each farmer."
   Source: rankings and underlying factors can be recovered only "if the sample of farmers is large enough" and extra information is collected, such as why one lot beats another and trial conditions. Preference learning methods are "also behind" online shopping recommendation systems (p. 104).
   Replacement: "If enough farmers take part and give some extra information, such as why one seed lot is better than another, methods from preference learning, which also lie behind online shopping recommendation systems, could rebuild a ranking of varieties and find the factors that shape farmer preferences, using each farmer's location to judge environmental adaptation."

7. **Reason for triads is slightly shifted (Imprecise, low).**
   Card: "Triadic comparisons are chosen because they are simple for farmers and give more information than pairs."
   Source: the main text says the evaluation must keep noise in the data to a minimum and that keeping things simple is key, then proposes triads (p. 104). Footnote 1 says triads give more information than pairs, but pairs would in principle also work (p. 109).
   Replacement: "Triadic comparisons are proposed to keep the task simple and the data less noisy, and a footnote adds that triads give more information than pairs, although pairs would in principle also work."

8. **Scaling sentence is a little stronger than the source (Imprecise, low).**
   Card: "Participatory plant breeding, participatory variety selection, community seed banks and seed fairs have produced valuable lessons over two decades, but none has been scaled up to reach large numbers of farmers."
   Source: there has been "no breakthrough in upscaling" these projects. The list also includes small-scale organised seed production projects (p. 102). The abstract says they have not been "widely scaled up".
   Replacement: "Participatory plant breeding, participatory variety selection, community seed banks, seed fairs and small-scale seed production projects have produced valuable lessons over two decades, but none has been widely scaled up to reach large numbers of farmers."

9. **Add the main uses of the results (omission, medium to high for tricot users).** Insert at the end of Summary paragraph 2:
   "The results would guide which varieties to distribute the next year, reveal clusters of farmers with different preferences, and support predictions for places where a variety has not yet been tested."
   And in paragraph 4, after the sentence on input subsidies: "Seed companies could also have their own varieties evaluated, which might help finance the network."
   With fixes 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 the Summary is about 460 words, inside the 400 to 500 target.

10. **Strengthen not_established (low).** Append:
   "; that blind evaluation makes farmer reports more objective; that variety performance can be predicted for places not yet tested; that subsidising information improves food security more than subsidising physical inputs"
