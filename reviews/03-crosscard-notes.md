# Cross-card notes

Facts that two or more cards state differently, found while reviewing the cards against their sources. Each item says what the sources say, how it was settled, and where.

Updated 2026-11-25.

## Open

### The 40% cost figure

- **Cards:** desousa-2024-asd, occelli-2024-agecon, vanetten-2020-rtbwp.
- **What the sources say:** de Sousa et al. 2024 (p. 6) says tricot was about 40% cheaper than RCBD in Central America and cites Occelli et al. 2024. Occelli et al. 2024 makes one cost comparison only, tricot-PVS against group-PVS: 28% lower total cost and 62% lower cost per farmer (p. 275, Table A7). It has no RCBD comparison and no 40% figure. van Etten et al. 2020 (RTB working paper, p. 21) gives a rough estimate of about 40% lower cost against intensive conventional PVS, with no data.
- **Likely explanation:** the 40% in de Sousa et al. 2024 comes from the RTB working paper estimate, combined with the Occelli citation and an RCBD comparison that neither source makes.
- **Status:** waiting for confirmation from an author of de Sousa et al. 2024. The three cards already describe each source's own figure in their Notes. Once confirmed, make the wording match across the three cards.

## Settled

### Nicaragua trial years

- **Cards:** vanetten-2019-pnas, desousa-2023-joss.
- **What the sources say:** van Etten et al. 2019 gives only an overall window, 2012 to 2016. de Sousa et al. 2023 (JOSS) says the five Nicaragua seasons ran from 2015 to 2017.
- **Settled by:** SI Appendix Table S1 of van Etten et al. 2019, supplied by the curator. It lists the five Nicaragua seasons in 2015 and 2016 (842 plots), Ethiopia in 2013 to 2015 and India in 2012 to 2015. The JOSS dates are flagged as a source issue. Fixed in PR #30.

### The Ulzen citation

- **Cards:** nabateregga-2025-eja, ulzen-2025-agrirxiv.
- **What the sources say:** Nabateregga et al. 2025 cites "Ulzen et al. (2024)" for farmer factors affecting heap volume estimates.
- **Settled by:** the curator confirmed that this reference is the agriRxiv preprint carded as ulzen-2025-agrirxiv, and that the citation is in error. Fixed in PR #31.

### Plots or farmers

- **Cards:** desousa-2024-asd, cairns-2026-jxb.
- **What the sources say:** de Sousa et al. 2024 says tricot reached about 150,000 farmer-managed plots. Cairns et al. 2026 cites it as over 150,000 farmers.
- **Settled:** the error is in Cairns et al. 2026. Plots and farmers are not the same, since one farmer can host more than one plot. desousa-2024-asd reports plots and lists the farmer reading under not_established. cairns-2026-jxb notes that the source reports plots. No change needed.

### Genebank panel size

- **Cards:** mancini-2017-scirep, fadda-2020-fps.
- **What the sources say:** Mancini et al. 2017 gives 400 accessions, 25 of them improved varieties and the rest landraces. Fadda et al. 2020 gives 373 farmer varieties and 27 improved varieties for the same panel.
- **Settled:** left as it is, as a minor point. Each card reports its own source's numbers and notes the difference. Mancini's Supplementary Table S9 or an author could resolve it if needed.
