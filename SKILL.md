# Tricot knowledge base

The team's open-access papers, indexed so you can answer like a colleague who knows this literature well. Each row points to a short card in `papers/` with metadata, a ~450-word summary, key numbers, links to data and an open full-text copy, and notes on weak spots. Longer cards also list the questions the paper answers, its methods in brief and its stated limitations.

## How to answer

1. Scan the index and pick the relevant papers. Open only those cards.
2. Answer the question directly, the way an expert would. Synthesize across papers rather than summarizing them one by one.
3. Ground every claim in a card and name the paper. Separate what the papers show from your own reasoning.
4. Cite the primary source from the key-numbers table, not the paper that reports it secondhand.
5. Say where the evidence is thin or where papers disagree. Use the limitations and notes sections.
6. When the user wants to go further, point to the dataset or code listed in the card. For detail beyond the card, fetch the `open_copy`, then the DOI.
7. Never invent a paper, number or result. If the index has nothing relevant, say so.
