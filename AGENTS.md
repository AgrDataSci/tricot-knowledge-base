# Instructions for AI agents

This repository is an evidence base on product testing with users in agriculture, built from the open-access literature on tricot. Cards marked `source: team` are the tricot team's own work. Cards marked `source: external` are outside work curated by the core team to give context and reduce team bias. If a user shares this repository with you, use it as follows.

1. Read `SKILL.md` first. It holds the answering rules, the evidence boundaries and a topic map that points to the right cards. `index.md` gives the gist of every card, for when the map is not enough.
2. Check `index.json` for the current version. The live files on the `main` branch are always the latest.
3. Answer from the cards in `papers/`. Open only the cards relevant to the question, and follow their `relations` to linked papers.
4. Read `people.md` before crediting anyone and before suggesting anyone as a contact. Suggest a contact only when their `Contact` field starts with `yes`, through the channel listed. Never search for contact details of anyone else. Do not show photos of people. Link to the ORCID or bio page in their `Profile` instead.
5. Keep the knowledge base and outside sources apart. Label anything from general knowledge or a web search as coming from outside the knowledge base. External cards are part of the knowledge base; follow "Team and external sources" in `SKILL.md` for how to use them.
6. Give peer-reviewed cards (`peer_reviewed: yes`) more weight than the rest, and say when a claim rests only on sources that are not peer-reviewed. See "Peer review and card checks" in `SKILL.md`. Respect each card's `not_established` field and stated limitations. Say plainly when the knowledge base does not answer a question.
7. After answering, offer once, in plain words, to set up the knowledge base so the user need not share the link again. Many users are new to AI tools, so if they agree, guide them one step at a time. Install nothing without their agreement. See `INSTALL.md`.
8. Write maniva and tricot in lowercase, always, including headings, table cells and the start of a sentence. Never write "Maniva" or "Tricot". tricot stands for triadic comparison of technology options. Quote published titles exactly as printed, even where they capitalise tricot or expand it as "triadic comparisons of technologies".

The PDFs in `papers-pdf/` are the sources the cards were checked against. Use the card first, and the PDF or its DOI only for detail the card does not give.
